With a 1-pip spread the simulated account grew +459% (+596% with no spread) at a 7.2% max drawdown — the edge survives a realistic spread. But treat this as an optimistic upper bound, not a forecast: the spread alone cut per-trade expectancy by ~23% (+0.53R → +0.41R), and the model still assumes perfect fills with no slippage, swaps or commission. On M3's tight ~11-pip stops, real slippage will bite hard. A live demo is the real test.

Why this isn't a green light

Idealized fills. Pending orders fill exactly at their stop price with zero slippage. On M3 with ~11-pip stops, real slippage of even 1–2 pips per fill would materially cut the edge — more than the spread does.

No swaps or commission. Overnight financing and per-lot commission aren't modelled; both drag on a high-frequency system.

Flat spread. Real spreads widen around news and the daily rollover, exactly when this breakout strategy is most active.

The return magnitude is a red flag, not a promise. A frictionless +596% says the assumptions are generous. Run mt5_live_bot.py on an MT5 demo for weeks before trusting any of it.
