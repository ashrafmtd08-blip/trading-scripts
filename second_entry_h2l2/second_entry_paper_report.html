With realistic costs (a 1-pip spread and 0.5-pip slippage per fill) the simulated account grew +322% (+596% with no costs) at a 8.5% max drawdown — the edge survives, but thinner. Treat it as an upper bound, not a forecast: costs cut per-trade expectancy by ~46% (+0.53R → +0.28R), and the model still assumes a flat spread with no swaps, commission or variable news-time spread. On M3's tight ~11-pip stops these bite hard. A live demo is the real test.

Why this isn't a green light

Flat spread + fixed slippage. This models 1 pip of spread and 0.5 pip of slippage per fill — but real spread widens around news and rollover, exactly when this breakout strategy fires, and stop fills can slip much more in fast moves.

No swaps or commission. Overnight financing and per-lot commission aren't modelled; both drag on a high-frequency system.

One vendor feed, one spread. Your broker's bid/ask and fills will differ — sometimes materially — from this single historical series.

The return magnitude is still generous, not a promise. Even after costs, +322% assumes flawless execution. Run mt5_live_bot.py (or the EA in the Strategy Tester) on demo for weeks before trusting any of it.
